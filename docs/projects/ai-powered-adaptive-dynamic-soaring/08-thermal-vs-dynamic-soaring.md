# 08 — Thermal vs. Dynamic Soaring

**Discussion date:** 2026-10-18. **Status:** conceptual comparison and candidate experiments, not validated results.

## Key distinction

- **Thermal soaring (eagles, vultures, gliders):** circles inside rising air, often generated by solar heating of land. The airmass itself carries the aircraft upward.
- **Dynamic soaring (albatrosses):** crosses a spatial gradient in *horizontal wind velocity* while climbing, turning and descending to harvest energy from wind shear.
- **Ridge soaring:** uses updrafts created by wind deflected upward over terrain; not automatically dynamic soaring.
- **Wave-slope soaring:** uses upward airflow deflected over ocean waves; can coexist with dynamic soaring and must not be mistaken for wind-shear extraction.

Example: if a glider sinks at 1 m/s in the local airmass and its thermal rises at 3 m/s, approximate ground-relative climb is 2 m/s. **Illustrative numbers, not project measurements.** Dynamic soaring instead requires a suitable velocity difference between air layers, not merely strong uniform wind.

| Feature | Thermal soaring | Dynamic soaring |
|---|---|---|
| Inspiration | Eagles, vultures, frigatebirds | Albatrosses |
| Source of useful energy | Rising air | Wind shear / velocity gradient |
| Typical maneuver | Circle within thermal; climb; glide elsewhere | Repeated climb across gradient, bank/turn, descend and repeat |
| Environment | Sun-heated land and convection | Windy ocean boundary layer / suitable shear |
| AI challenge | Detect, center and predict drifting thermals | Estimate uncertain shear and optimize safe energy-positive maneuvers |
| CWSF outdoor demonstration | Comparatively feasible with glider/autopilot | More difficult: glide ratio, turn losses, suitable wind and safety |
| Classical benchmark | ArduSoar thermalling | Optimized dynamic-soaring trajectories |

## Engineering options

**Thermal-soaring variant:** instrument FW-01 or a suitable glider with barometer/variometer, airspeed sensing, and flight logs. Compare fixed search, conventional thermal centering (e.g. ArduSoar), and RL controller using repeated simulated thermals and safely controlled physical trials. Measure thermal acquisition, climb rate, usable energy/time, failures and generalization.

**Current dynamic-soaring variant:** wind-shear flight model with measured aerodynamic polar; classical optimized soaring baseline; AI adaptive policy using realistic wind estimates; net mechanical energy per complete matched cycle, endurance, electrical consumption and safety constraints. No proof yet that FW-01 could sustain dynamic soaring outdoors.

**Optional longer-term synthesis:** a supervisor chooses thermal soaring, dynamic soaring, conventional glide or powered flight based on observations, confidence, mission energy and risk. Useful research direction but significantly harder to implement and validate; **not** part of current core deliverable.
